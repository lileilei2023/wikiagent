title: IP孵化 (IP Incubation)
---
## Definition
IP incubation refers to the process of building, operating and monetizing personal or brand IP (Intellectual Property) with specific personas, professional capabilities and fan influence through content output, operation planning and commercialization design.

## Core types
- Personal IP: including knowledge IP, entrepreneur IP, talent IP, expert IP, etc.
- Brand IP: including enterprise brand IP, product IP, virtual IP, etc.
- Content IP: including drama IP, column IP, activity IP, etc.

## Core stages
1. **Positioning stage**: Determine the IP persona, target audience, differentiated value, and monetization path
2. **Content stage**: Produce and distribute content that matches the persona to accumulate fans and trust
3. **Operation stage**: Interact with fans, operate private domains, and improve fan stickiness and conversion rate
4. **Monetization stage**: Realize commercialization through advertising, live streaming with goods, knowledge payment, training, etc.

## Core success factors
- Clear and consistent persona
- Sustainable high-quality content output capability
- Matching monetization path and product system
- Long-term operation thinking, not pursuing short-term traffic
