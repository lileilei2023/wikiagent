title: 私域运营 (Private Domain Operation)
---
## Definition
Private domain operation refers to the business model of operating owned user traffic (usually on WeChat personal accounts, enterprise WeChat, official accounts, mini programs, independent Apps) to achieve repeated monetization through refined operation, user relationship maintenance, and trust building. It is opposed to public domain traffic operation that relies on platform advertising and algorithm recommendation.

## Core characteristics
- Owned traffic assets, no need to pay platform fees repeatedly for reaching users
- High user trust, high conversion rate and repurchase rate
- Suitable for high customer unit price, high repurchase, high decision threshold products
- Core indicators: user LTV (lifetime value), repurchase rate, private domain diversion rate, conversion rate

## Core methodologies
- High-value user layering operation: 20% of high-value users contribute 80% of profits, focus resources on core users
- IP-based trust building: build personal/brand IP through content such as Moments, short videos, live broadcasts
- Full-link operation: combine diversion, conversion, repurchase, referral to form a closed business loop
- Supply chain expansion: expand product categories based on user needs to improve user LTV
