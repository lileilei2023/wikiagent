## Basic information
- Founded in 2013, listed on NYSE in 2020
- Global retail chain brand, entered 100+ countries and regions, 5000+ global stores as of end of 2021
- Core product strategy: originally "three highs and three lows" (high appearance, high quality, high efficiency; low cost, low gross margin, low price), now upgraded to focus on "interest consumption"

## Core competitive advantages
1. **Product power**: Co-brand with 80+ global IPs, launch about 550 new SKUs per month, covering 11 major categories
2. **Supply chain capacity**: 900+ global suppliers, "factory direct to store" mode, fast response to market changes
3. **Innovative franchise mode**: fully managed, asset-light partner franchise mode, partners do not bear inventory and operation risks

## Private domain operation practice
- 10 million+ private domain users accumulated in 2 years (from 2019 to 2021)
- Four-stage private domain operation system:
  1. 1.0 stage: extensive activity-based user acquisition, basic user tagging and interaction
  2. 2.0 stage: community pyramid model, divided into regional groups, category groups, KOC groups for differentiated operation
  3. 3.0 stage: life cycle-based community operation, personalized touch according to user joining period and tags
  4. 4.0 stage: user co-creation, involve users in product development, product testing, marketing communication
