## Summary
- Compiles 50 real-world monetization cases across 7 major categories: private domain operation, IP incubation, short video operations, live streaming operations, brand operation, traffic acquisition, and industry service providers
- Covers vertical industries including beauty, FMCG, health care, education, retail, fashion, jewelry, maternal and infant, food and beverage, cultural collections, etc.
- Provides actionable methodologies, quantitative data (revenue scale, ROI, user volume, conversion rates) and best practices from frontline industry practitioners
- Core insight: Combination of Douyin public domain traffic acquisition + private domain refined operation + IP trust building is the most stable monetization model for most businesses in the current market
- Highlights the trend of specialization in the Douyin ecosystem: MCN, DP, traffic providers,团长 (group buying leaders) and other roles have formed a complete industrial chain
- Emphasizes long-term thinking: high LTV user operation, IP long-term incubation, brand asset accumulation are core competitive advantages to resist cycle fluctuations
- Notes that the current market dividend of extensive traffic play has disappeared, and refined operation, product power and supply chain capacity have become the core thresholds

## Notable data points
- 4 million private domain fans can generate annual revenue of over 100 million yuan with high customer unit price products
- Douyin public to private domain diversion stable conversion rate is about 3-5% for mature operations
- The 2/8 principle applies to private domain operations: 20% of high-value users contribute 80% of the profit
- The average CPM of high-quality entertainment content accounts on Douyin is about 20-80 yuan, while the CPM of vertical medical accounts can reach thousands of yuan
- High-end users' private domain LTV can reach more than 100,000 yuan, and the user life cycle can exceed 7 years
- The success rate of Douyin IP incubation is about 1%, requiring continuous content output and operation capabilities
